If a video shows a public figure promoting an investment, assume it is fake. The Prime Minister does not sell crypto platforms.

A short recording is enough to clone a voice. A code word defeats the "grandchild in trouble" call.

No bank, police service or government agency asks for payment this way.

Fake gains are the hook

A dashboard showing your money growing is the scam. Try a small withdrawal before investing more.

04 // Legal processAI in the cloud

If the suspect used ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or similar, the chats sit on the provider's servers. Most providers are US companies. Move early.

  1. Preserve first

    Preservation demand under s. 487.012 Cr.C., and a voluntary preservation request to the provider. OpenAI's published policy preserves data for up to 90 days on request. Deleted chats can be gone from some providers within 30 days.

  2. Then compel

    Production order (s. 487.014) for Canadian-held data. US-held content usually needs a mutual legal assistance request through Justice Canada's International Assistance Group, or the provider's own process. Involve your Crown Attorney's office early, and check each provider's law-enforcement guidelines.

  3. Ask for the right things

    Subscriber and payment details, IP and login history, chat content, uploaded files, generated images, and any safety flags or account enforcement.

  4. Imminent harm

    Providers run emergency disclosure processes for threats to life. A provider referral is possible but never guaranteed; no Canadian law requires AI companies to report threats.

Where to send it: major AI providers

Verified law-enforcement portals and legal-process pages. Each needs a government email and valid process; all run emergency channels for threats to life. Confirm the current address on the provider's own page before serving.

  • OpenAI — ChatGPTGovernment Data Request Policy; submissions via the Kodex portal
  • Anthropic — ClaudeRequest by email to the law-enforcement address on the support page
  • Google — GeminiLaw Enforcement Request System (LERS) portal
  • MetaLaw Enforcement Online Request System

Reference: OpenAI Government User Data Request Policy (PDF). Not legal advice; confirm with your service's legal services or the Crown.

05 // Threat intelDark AI tool watchlist

Criminal AI brands come and go. Most are an uncensored open model or a jailbroken mainstream AI under a new name. Follow the payments and accounts, not the brand.

WormGPT

2023–26

The original "ChatGPT without limits". Version 4 sold on Telegram for $50/month or $220 lifetime. New versions keep appearing.

FraudGPT

2023

Dark web and Telegram, $200/month. Pitched for phishing, business email compromise and carding.

KawaiiGPT

2025

Free on GitHub, runs in minutes, even on Android. Wrote bank phishing emails and data-theft scripts in testing.

Luciferus

Aug 2026

Exploit forum, $35–$75/month. Claims its own 120B model; Sophos assesses an uncensored open model. Produced a remote-access trojan.

MessiahGPT

Oct 2026

BreachForums, from $8 after free queries, with a website and Telegram group. Marketed for ransomware and phishing kits.

APEX AI

2026

Sold as self-hosted software, so every buyer runs a private copy. No central server to seize.

Nytheon AI

2025

Tor-only platform bundling several uncensored Llama-based models. Promoted on Telegram and Russian-language forums.

DIG AI

2025–26

Tor, no account needed. Promoted on underground markets alongside drugs and stolen financial data.

Autonomous attack agents

2026–

The shift from text to action: AI agents that run the whole attack, not just draft it. Artex, an open-source AI pen-test framework, was pulled by its author but survives in 12,000+ forks, reportedly used against 200+ organizations. Lowers the skill needed to launch a serious attack. Some forks are booby-trapped — do not download and run.

Sources: Palo Alto Unit 42, Sophos CTU, Trellix, Cato CTRL, Resecurity. Prices and capabilities are seller claims.

07 // TermsGlossary
Large language model (LLM)
AI that writes text by predicting the next word. Fluent is not the same as true.
Hallucination
A confident, invented answer: fake quotes, case law or facts.
Agent
AI that takes actions (browses, clicks, runs code, sends email) toward a goal, not just answers.
Local model
A model downloaded and run on the user's own computer. No provider sees the prompts; evidence stays on the device.
Open-weight model
A model whose files are published for anyone to download (Llama, Qwen, Gemma, DeepSeek).
Uncensored
An open model retrained so it no longer refuses harmful requests.
Abliterated
An open model with its "refusal direction" removed directly from the weights. Cheap, fast, and thousands are on Hugging Face.
Jailbreak
A prompt that tricks a mainstream AI into ignoring its safety rules, e.g. "it's just a test" or "it's fiction".
GGUF
A common file format for local language models (.gguf), used by LM Studio, Ollama and llama.cpp.
Safetensors
A file format for model weights (.safetensors), common for image models and LoRAs.
LoRA
A small add-on file that teaches an image model one face, style or object. A LoRA named after a real person is significant.
Deepfake
Media where a real person's face or voice is generated or swapped in.
Voice clone
A synthetic copy of a real voice made from a short recording.
Content Credentials (C2PA)
A signed record attached to a file describing how it was made or edited. Its absence proves nothing.
SynthID
Google's invisible watermark in AI images, audio and video from its own tools.
Prompt injection
Hidden instructions in a web page or document that hijack an AI agent reading it.
Hugging Face
The largest public library of AI models. Legitimate, and also where most uncensored models are published.
Sextortion
Threatening to share intimate images, real or AI-made, to get money or more images. A Criminal Code offence since July 2026 (Bill C-16).
MFIPPA / FIPPA
Ontario's municipal and provincial access and privacy laws. MFIPPA covers municipal police services; FIPPA covers the OPP.
IPC
Information and Privacy Commissioner of Ontario, the privacy oversight body for Ontario police.

No terms match. Try a shorter word.
