# AI Toolkit for Investigators

A one-page field reference for police investigators in Ontario, Canada, that accompanies an AI technology briefing:

- verification checklists for photos, videos, calls and documents
- where AI evidence lives on a seized device
- legal process for AI providers
- a watchlist of dark AI tools
- a glossary
- Ontario law and oversight (Bill C-16, IPC Ontario, court AI practice directions)
- reporting contacts

Live page: https://westhollandgoods.github.io/AITOOLKIT/

General reference only. Not legal advice and not a service policy.
